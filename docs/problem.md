# Problem statement: job search tracker

## Who it's for
Me, during an active job search, juggling interviews, applications,
networking and upskilling at the same time.

## The problem
- My weeks are ad hoc. Whatever is most urgent takes all my energy,
  and I feel guilty about every channel I'm not working on.
- My current tracker counts activities, but activity isn't progress.
  High numbers with no interviews feel like wasted time; an interview
  week looks like a week of missed targets.
- The weekly numbers aren't linked to specific applications, so I
  can't tell what's actually working.
- My real list of opportunities lives in my head. My Notion table
  goes stale because updating it is easy to forget.
- Nothing defines "enough," so the search expands into my personal
  life, and I'm at risk of burning out.

## What the tracker should help me do
1. **Put my energy where it matters most.** An opportunity's value:
   - Alignment with my direction is a gate.
   - Then: referral over cold application, further along over
     earlier, closer deadline over later.
2. **Know when I've done enough for the week,** adjusted to my energy,
   so I can rest without guilt. A high-value event like an interview
   can rightly take over a week.
3. **Learn what works.** Compare direct channels (referrals, events,
   cold applications) by what leads to interviews. Track indirect
   investments (upskilling, side projects, LinkedIn) by whether I'm
   keeping them up. Give me an honest signal, not false precision,
   and ideas for what to try.

## Constraints
- Logging takes seconds, on my phone or laptop, whenever things happen.
- Catching up on late updates is easy and guilt-free.
- The tracker lowers stress. It never adds to it.

## What success looks like in a month
- My weeks feel sustainable: working toward the goal, not excessively.
- I end the week knowing I did what I could, given my energy.
- I've learned something real about what works in my search.

## Signs it has failed
- I've stopped using it.
- It causes more stress than it relieves.
- It hasn't told me anything I didn't already know.
